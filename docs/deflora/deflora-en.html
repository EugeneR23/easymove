What is wrong with the pages you do have

Fourteen city pages exist, but inside they carry the same text with the name swapped. Google recognises that and mostly stops showing it. AI services will not quote it at all, because there is nothing there to quote.

Fix first

An entire city with the name misspelled

There are two Pembroke Pines pages on your site: the correct one, and one spelled Pembroke Pnes. The error is not buried in a URL. It sits in the page title, in the heading, and in the body text the customer reads. Your own "Where We Deliver" page links to it.

Two pages compete for one town, and some of your visitors land on the one where their city is spelled wrong. That is a trust problem before it is a ranking problem.

06

AI search: the door is open, the room is empty

More people now ask ChatGPT instead of Google: recommend a florist in Aventura who delivers today. The answer gets assembled from whatever the model could read. Good news, it is allowed in. Bad news, there is nothing to read.

Already good

AI crawlers are allowed on the site

Your settings explicitly permit GPTBot, which is ChatGPT, along with ClaudeBot and a dozen others. A lot of shops are accidentally blocked here and spend years not understanding why they never appear in answers. Yours is set up correctly. Leave it.

Fix first

No card for AI to read the facts from

There is a simple file called llms.txt. It says in plain language who you are, where you are, which areas you deliver to, how late you take an order for same day delivery, what delivery costs, what you do for Shabbat and the Jewish holidays, and how to order a corporate subscription.

A model takes those facts ready made. Without the file it digs them out of your markup, gets your hours and prices wrong, or simply prefers the competitor who has one. We checked three possible addresses. None of them exists.
